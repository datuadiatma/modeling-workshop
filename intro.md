# Welcome

## Young - Owens Group | Fall 2026 Numerical Modeling Internal Workshop

Prepared by __Datu Adiatma__ ([yadiatma@fsu.edu](mailto:yadiatma@fsu.edu))

__Dept. of Earth Ocean & Atmospheric Science, FSU__

---

This website contains notes and activities for the Fall 2026 Young - Owens group workshop/discussion on numerical modeling.

The goal of this sesssion is to give a broad overview of numerical modeling (especially geochemical box modeling), and an introduction to Python programming.

This mini workshop is not intended to provide the most comprehensive view to numerical modeling and Python programming. Please take a look at the additional resources section to find references and extra resources associated with this topic.

:::{caution} Disclaimer
:class: dropdown
I will dedicate around 4 hrs/week to prepare the materials for this workshop and (more than likely) do the bulk of the work on Wednesday or Thursday night before our group meeting. Therefore, the content  of this site is subject to change until Friday Morning 9 AM Eastern Time.

There will be plenty of typos and grammatical errors throughout this site. I hope they don't distract you, but please let me know when you find them. I will fix them as we go.
:::
